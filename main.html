Vizag, India


Vizag, officially known as Visakhapatnam, is a beautiful coastal city in Andhra Pradesh, India. Renowned for its pristine beaches, rich cultural heritage, and scenic hills, Vizag offers a perfect blend of relaxation and adventure for every traveller.

  1. Simhachalam Temple — A magnificent temple dedicated to Lord Narasimha, perched on a hill with stunning architectural grandeur.
  2. Araku Valley — A picturesque hilly retreat known for its waterfalls, coffee plantations, and scenic views.
  3. Beaches of Vizag — Including the famous Rushikonda and Yarada Beach, offering serene coastal experiences.
  4. Kailasagiri Hill Park — A popular hilltop park providing panoramic views of the city and the Bay of Bengal.
  5. Borra Caves — Stunning limestone caves with spectacular stalactite and stalagmite formations.
  • Andhra Meals — A traditional feast featuring spicy rice, lentil curry, and assorted pickles.
  • Palakaya — A beloved local delicacy of deep-fried fish, a Vizag specialty.
  • Pesarattu — A crispy green gram dosa that is a breakfast staple in the region.
  • Bandaru Laddu — A popular sweet made with flour and ghee, originating from the coastal region.
  • Royyala Vepudu — A famous shrimp fry dish bursting with coastal flavours.
